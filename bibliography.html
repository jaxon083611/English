Billy the Kid: An Annotated Bibliography

References

Anderson, Hugh Allen. “The Life and Legend of Billy the Kid: A Historical Overview.” TSHA Online, 1 May 1995, https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/mccarty-henry. Accessed 28 Jan. 2026.

Anderson’s article provides a detailed historical overview of Billy the Kid’s life and the development of his legend. It discusses the uncertainty surrounding his early life, his involvement in the Lincoln County War, and the events that led to his death. The article also separates fact from myth, explaining how the stories about him changed over time. Anderson is associated with the Texas State Historical Association, which makes this source reliable and historically grounded. This source is useful because it gives accurate background information and places Billy the Kid within the context of the American Southwest.

“Billy the Kid.” Biography.com, 3 Apr. 2014, https://www.biography.com/crime/a64432952/outlaw-billy-the-kid. Accessed 28 Jan. 2026.

This article offers a clear and accessible summary of Billy the Kid’s life for a general audience. It focuses on important events such as his youth, criminal activity, and involvement in the Lincoln County War. The article also emphasizes his reputation and the way his story has been remembered in American culture. While the source is not as detailed as academic works, it provides helpful basic information and is useful for understanding how Billy the Kid is portrayed in popular media.

Britannica Editors. “Billy the Kid.” Encyclopedia Britannica, 12 Dec. 2025, https://www.britannica.com/biography/Billy-the-Kid-American-outlaw. Accessed 28 Jan. 2026.

This encyclopedia entry provides a balanced and factual account of Billy the Kid’s life. It includes verified details about his early years, criminal activities, and death, while avoiding exaggeration or legend. Because Encyclopedia Britannica is a respected reference source, the information is reliable and carefully researched. This source is especially useful for confirming key facts, dates, and historical events related to Billy the Kid.

“FAMOUS CRIMINALS: WHO IS BILLY THE KID?” New Jersey Criminal Law Attorney, https://www.newjerseycriminallawattorney.com/legal-articles-information/all-about-billy-the-kid-and-other-famous-criminals-of-the-wild-west/. Accessed 28 Jan. 2026.

This article discusses Billy the Kid as one of the most famous criminals of the Wild West and focuses on his legal status and crimes. It provides insight into how he was viewed as an outlaw during his lifetime and why his reputation continues today. Although the source is not purely academic, it offers a useful legal perspective on his actions and the consequences he faced. This perspective helps explain why Billy the Kid became a symbol of lawlessness in the American West.

Gardner, Mark Lee. “Billy the Kid: From Outlaw to Legend.” American Heritage, 2018, https://www.americanheritage.com/billy-kid-outlaw-legend. Accessed 28 Jan. 2026.

Gardner’s article examines how Billy the Kid transformed from a real historical figure into a cultural legend. It explores the role of newspapers, books, and films in shaping his image and discusses why people remain fascinated by outlaws. Gardner is a respected historian, and his work is based on strong historical research. This source is valuable because it explains how myths develop and how public memory can differ from historical reality.

“The Life and Legend of Billy the Kid.” PBS.org, https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/americanexperience/features/billy-life-and-legend-billy-kid/. Accessed 28 Jan. 2026.

This PBS source presents Billy the Kid’s story in the context of the American Experience documentary. It combines historical facts with storytelling to explain the environment of the Old West and the events that shaped his life. The article also examines how his legend grew over time. Because PBS is known for educational and well-researched content, this source is reliable and useful for both factual and interpretive information.

“9 Things You May Not Know about Billy the Kid.” History, A&E Television Networks, 14 July 2016, https://www.history.com/articles/9-things-you-may-not-know-about-billy-the-kid. Accessed 28 Jan. 2026.

While it is written for a general audience, it is supported by historical research and credible information. This source is helpful for adding interesting details and keeping readers or listeners engaged.
